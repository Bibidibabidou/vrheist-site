Design rules for the whole building

1 · One home corner

The lift is always the NE 2 × 2 m corner. Players learn it on floor B1 and it becomes their safe zone, the place to regroup, and the point where the experience recalibrates. The lift leaves only once every player is inside it.

2 · Respect the real room

Virtual outer walls sit on the physical 6 m boundary. Players should never have to stand closer than 40 cm to the real wall (the dashed line on the plans). Wall-mounted things such as the fuse box, key switches and keypad sit on consoles or columns that stick out about 40 cm, so nobody lunges into a real wall.

3 · Never the same room twice

Each floor uses a different layout type: rooms (B1), a loop (G), islands (1), a room inside a room (2) and an open edge (Roof). Players then feel they've moved through a whole building, not reloaded one box.

4 · Borrow space you can't walk

Every floor has a view out past the boundary: the sewer tunnel, the atrium balcony, the guard corridor window, the depth of the vault, the skyline, and a glass skylight. This is the cheapest way to make 36 m² feel like a museum.

5 · Use the vertical axis

With leg and hand tracking, height becomes gameplay: step over low lasers, duck under high ones, crouch behind cases to hide, reach high for a painting, look down through glass.

6 · Nobody can do it alone

Every floor has at least one task that needs two players far apart (≥ 2.5 m) or out of each other's sight. Players have to talk, which is what the multi-user criterion rewards.

7 · One alarm meter, carried through

A 3-strike alarm meter is shared across every floor. Touching a laser or being seen by a guard adds a strike. It raises the stakes floor by floor and changes the rooftop ending.

8 · Optional: one physical anchor prop

Ties to the props & trackers lecture. A real 60 × 60 × 90 cm plinth stays fixed at (1.8 m, 1.8 m) and is dressed differently on each floor: planning table → sculpture → showcase → prize pedestal → supply crate. Players can actually touch it on every floor.

The building & the tension curve

The lift: how you "teleport" without teleporting

Spec

2 × 2 m car in the NE corner, with a 1.2 m door on its south face. Inside: a floor panel (only the next floor is lit), a handrail, a floor counter, and the alarm meter. It fits 2–4 players standing.

Ride sequence (~8 s)

All players are inside the trigger volume → doors close → rumble, hum and a ticking counter → the next floor's sublevel streams in behind closed doors → doors open on a new world. There's no fade to black, because the closed car hides the swap.

Why it is legal

The brief forbids personal teleport because it breaks co-location. Here nobody moves: the whole level swaps around everyone at once, so every player's real position still matches their virtual one.

Unreal implementation notes

Level structure

L_Heist_Persistent holds the lift, players, GameState (alarm meter, loot score, code fragments) and the anchor plinth. Streaming sublevels: L_B1_Hideout · L_G_Lasers · L_1_Treasure · L_2_Vault · L_R_Roof. The lift loads the next sublevel and unloads the old one while its doors are shut.

Reuse what's already in L_Heist1

BP_Laser → every beam (extend it with a reflection trace for the mirror). BP_SquareButton → lift panel, big button, palm scanner. BP_Counter → loot score and countdown. BP_GrabPickupBase / PinchPickupBase → loot, tools, mirror. BP_LobbySessionSelector → stays in the AR staging area.

Server authority

The lift trigger, pressure plates, key-turn timing and alarm meter all run on the server and replicate out. A two-player check (two keys turned within 0.5 s, or two different players' palms on the scanner) must compare player IDs, not just count hands.

Optional 3 × 6 m staging area

Use the extra AR strip as the crew van: players put on headsets, join the session, see each other in passthrough, then walk into the 6 × 6 room and spawn on the B1 onboarding pad.

Plans are drawn 1 m = 1 grid square. Coordinates are in metres from the NW corner. Adjust the lift corner to match the real lab's door and guardian setup.
